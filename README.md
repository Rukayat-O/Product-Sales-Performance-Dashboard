Product-Sales-Performance-Analysis

This project presents an analysis of product sales and profitability across products, states, regions, and categories.

🔍 Overview

The Product Sales Performance Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales and profit performance.

It focuses on:

* Product performance
* Profitability
* Regional performance
* State-level performance
* Category trends
* Product rankings

The dashboard helps identify the products, regions, and states contributing most to business profitability.

🎯 Objectives

* Identify the highest-performing products.
* Analyze profit across states.
* Compare regional performance.
* Identify major profit contributors.
* Examine category trends.
* Highlight areas requiring improvement.

❓ Business Questions

* Which product generates the highest sales?
* Which state records the highest profit?
* Which region performs best?
* What products contribute most to sales?
* Are category sales increasing or declining?
* Which areas present opportunities for growth?

📚 Dataset Description

Dataset: Product sales and profitability data
Data Type: Sales and business performance data

Key Fields:

* Product
* Sales
* Profit
* State
* Region
* Category
* Time/Trend

🧹 Data Cleaning and Preparation

* Removed duplicate records.
* Standardized product and location names.
* Checked sales and profit values.
* Organized regional and state categories.
* Prepared product-level summaries.
* Created calculated metrics for dashboard analysis.

📈 Key Trends Discovered

* Total profit reached approximately $8.3M.
* Illinois recorded the highest state profit at approximately $833.7K.
* Instant Pot was the top-performing product at 31.52%.
* Nike Air Force 1 contributed 25.30%.
* East was the strongest region at 28.52%.
* The category trend showed a decline across the period analyzed.

📊 Dashboard Development

The dashboard includes:

* Total profit KPI
* Product performance
* State profit analysis
* Regional contribution
* Category trend
* Top-product ranking

💡 Recommendations

* Maintain strong inventory for top-performing products.
* Invest more in high-performing regions.
* Replicate successful strategies from Illinois.
* Promote weaker products and regions.
* Investigate the declining category trend.

Conclusion

The analysis reveals strong overall profitability, with Instant Pot, Illinois, and the East region emerging as key contributors. However, declining category performance highlights the need for targeted strategies to sustain growth.
